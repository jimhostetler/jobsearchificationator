## TIER 1: Core Searches (High Priority)

### Search A: Community Platforms (Genuine Connection)
Keywords: community platform + member engagement + direct support + genuine connection
Exclude: broadcast, algorithm-driven feeds, follower-based
Example matches: Circle, Mighty Networks, Lemmy, Bettermode, Tildes, Higher Logic

### Search B: Creator Platforms (Community-First, Not Monetization-First)
Keywords: creator platform + audience ownership + community tools + direct support community
Exclude: newsletter-only, tip-based, audience extraction models
Example matches: Ghost, Gumroad, BTS, Mighty Networks

### Search C: Education + Learning Communities
Keywords: education platform + learning community + peer support + skill-sharing + teacher empowerment
Exclude: SaaS corporate training, individual learning only
Example matches: Khan Academy, learning communities, peer tutoring platforms

### Search D: Local Organizing + Mutual Aid
Keywords: local organizing + mutual aid + community resilience + neighborhood + hyperlocal + skill-sharing networks
Exclude: hyperlocal delivery/commerce only (no genuine connection component)
Example matches: Nextdoor, Meetup, Peerspace, local organizing software, mutual aid platforms

### Search E: Clean Energy + Community Resilience
Keywords: clean energy + distributed energy + energy platform + community resilience + grid infrastructure
Exclude: fossil fuels, extractive models
Example matches: EnergyHub, DER management platforms, distributed energy infrastructure
